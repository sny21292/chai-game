Privacy Policy

Floor Pe Dhokha · last updated 3 November 2026

Floor Pe Dhokha ("the game") is made by Sunil Sharma, an independent developer. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, death count), your settings (music, sound, vibration) and anonymous play statistics (for example how many times you died on each level) are saved only in your browser or app storage on your own device. We do not run accounts, and this data is not sent to us unless you choose to share it. On CrazyGames, if you are logged in to a CrazyGames account, your progress and settings are also saved to that account by CrazyGames so you can continue on another device.

Advertising

When you play the game on CrazyGames, ads are shown by CrazyGames and its ad partners: an optional video you can choose to watch to skip a level, and occasional short breaks between levels. CrazyGames and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in the CrazyGames privacy policy. Consent for this is handled by CrazyGames where the law requires it. Played anywhere else (for example our own website), the game shows no ads.

Analytics

The game does not send analytics to us. Play statistics stay on your device; you can download them yourself from Settings and share them if you want to help us balance the levels. Platforms that host the game (such as CrazyGames) may measure play sessions under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

The web version has no purchases. If the mobile apps add a "remove ads" purchase, it will be handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we never see your payment details.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete all locally stored data at any time with Settings → Progress reset, by clearing your browser data, or by uninstalling the app.
