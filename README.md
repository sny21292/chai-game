# Floor Pe Dhokha

A desi troll platformer (the name means "the floor tricks you"). Guide **Chotu**, a cutting-chai glass, across a map of India where the floor, the spikes and even the door lie to you.

- Play: open `index.html` in a browser (no build step, no dependencies).
- Controls: ← → move, Space / ↑ jump, R restart, P pause, O settings. On the map: tap a state to select, tap again to enter; drag, pinch or scroll to zoom.
- States are stages, UTs are optional boss levels. Playable now: Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Kerala, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand, plus the Delhi and Chandigarh bosses (59 levels and 9 boss rooms in all).

Built with vanilla JS and canvas. Music and sound are synthesised with Web Audio.

Map data: Survey of India, via [DataMeet](https://github.com/datameet/maps).
